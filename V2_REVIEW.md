# RiskFi PT/YT V2 review

This is the active staker-owned yield implementation. V2 contracts are deployed and verified on Ethereum Sepolia and Arbitrum Sepolia; addresses and transaction receipts are in [v2-testnets.json](deployments/v2-testnets.json). The former V1 application code was removed from the active branch.

## Behavior

- [`RiskFiStakeVaultV2`](src/v2/RiskFiStakeVaultV2.sol) creates one reviewed report series. A deposit mints a 1:1 [`StakeReceiptREP`](src/v2/StakeReceiptREP.sol); staking burns REP and records a non-transferable, side-specific verdict position while minting the same notional amount of YT. Direct `stake` performs the deposit and REP burn in one transaction. Unstaked REP remains withdrawable and its collateral is excluded from losses, settlement, and dust sweeps. A winner claims principal plus 80% of the losing pool, the treasury receives 20% of the losing pool, and Void refunds principal. Principal loss scales payouts pro rata.
- [`StakerYieldToken`](src/v2/StakerYieldToken.sol) tracks a global yield index. Minting and transfers checkpoint balances so a later staker or buyer cannot claim earlier yield. Yield is paid independently of the report verdict.
- [`YieldCurveHookV2`](src/v2/YieldCurveHookV2.sol) owns YT/dUSD reserves and uses Uniswap v4 PoolManager for custom-accounting swaps. Its implied rate is `anchorRateWad + slopeWadPerUnit * netYTBought`, bounded by the configured minimum and maximum. Marginal YT price multiplies that rate by remaining years until maturity. `quoteBuy` and `quoteSell` use the same calculations as swaps. Quotes can become unavailable before maturity when a six-decimal payout rounds to zero or inventory/rate bounds are reached. The hook attributes YT income earned in its inventory to explicit LP shares.
- [`YieldCurveFactoryV2`](src/v2/YieldCurveFactoryV2.sol) requires the mined v4 permission bits and initializes the pool. [`StakeAndSellRouterV2`](src/v2/StakeAndSellRouterV2.sol) stakes dUSD, mints YT to itself, sells YT through the v4 router, and pays dUSD proceeds to the staker in one transaction. A failed swap reverts the entire stake.
- [`CCIPStakeSenderV2`](src/v2/CCIPStakeSenderV2.sol) sends funded `StakeIntentV2` messages through Chainlink's `IRouterClient` from Arbitrum Sepolia. [`CCIPStakeReceiverV2`](src/v2/CCIPStakeReceiverV2.sol) accepts only the configured router, chain, sender, and dUSD token, then credits a V2 position and mints staker-owned YT on Ethereum Sepolia. Each source payer has an independent nonce. Valid but late or expired deliveries become withdrawable REP for the source payer; undecodable messages and failed deposits use token recovery, with a designated operator when the payer cannot be decoded.

## Regression evidence

Run `forge test --match-path 'test/*V2.t.sol' --summary` for the 25 V2-only local tests; all passed at review time. They cover REP backing, local and CCIP-funded stakes, YT accrual, verdict settlement, quote/swap equality, 256 fuzzed round trips, time decay, rate bounds, maturity gates, slippage rollback, and mined hook deployment. The separate `V2LivePoolFork.t.sol` check passed against Sepolia's actual PoolManager using a local fork and the Tenderly public RPC. `forge fmt --check` and `forge build` are separate gates. See [V2_E2E.md](V2_E2E.md) for the complete local workflow.

[`EightVoterWorkflowV2.t.sol`](test/EightVoterWorkflowV2.t.sol) follows eight funded voters from signed report publication through five Yes and three No stakes (145/75 dUSD). Heidi's vote arrives through a mock CCIP router and gives her a V2 position and YT; a separate test sells her delivered YT through the v4 pool. Bob and Eve sell their newly minted YT during staking; Carol seeds the v4 pool with her YT and separate dUSD; Alice later buys YT. Two simulated yield periods distribute 33 dUSD to current YT holders and Carol's LP shares. A challenged No proposal is adjudicated Yes after maturity. All eight verdict positions are claimed, the treasury receives 15 dUSD from the losing pool, Carol exits LP after maturity, and principal rounding dust is swept after the claim window. The test accounts for every dUSD unit, including at most two base units of LP index rounding dust retained by the hook.

[`CCIPV2.t.sol`](test/CCIPV2.t.sol) checks independently reusable payer nonces, router-only delivery, replay rejection, wrong-chain rejection, late-delivery REP withdrawal by the source payer, and operator recovery for malformed messages. These are local mock-router tests. [`PreflightSourceV2.s.sol`](script/PreflightSourceV2.s.sol) makes a read-only live router fee quote for the V2 token-plus-data message shape; it sends no CCIP message.

The Arbitrum Sepolia sender quoted 744250004505817 wei for a live 5 dUSD V2 token-plus-data message and sent it under message ID `0xaed819aad1fde53644aa058481a0f606f0ae2e9f78a9bbf2a3264e8c4db1456d`. The quote varies with network conditions. The first destination attempt failed with an empty-data `ReceiverError`; a higher-gas manual execution succeeded. The receiver credited a 5 dUSD No position and minted 5 million YT base units. A later live yield injection paid 0.434802 dUSD to the wallet holding that delivered YT. See [deployment steps](DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Boundaries for review

- The vault uses **simulated yield injection** over DemoUSD. It is not an ERC-4626 lending integration, and DemoUSD is not Circle USDC.
- One V2 vault and YT series serves one report. Fungible YT across multiple reports sharing a maturity needs a separate bucket design and index-normalized accounting.
- V2 stakes and their Yes/No sides are public onchain. The non-transferable verdict position prevents secondary trading; it does not provide stake privacy.
- The V2 CCIP sender and receiver are deployed, and a live message reached `SUCCESS` on Ethereum Sepolia after a manual gas override. The deployed-state fork now requires `delivered(messageId) = true` and checks the funded position and YT. There is no World backend yet.
- The hook and atomic router are deployed and live buy/sell/atomic stake-and-sell transactions succeeded on Sepolia. A live 10 dUSD demo yield injection paid 9.999977 dUSD to the LP and 0.000021 dUSD to the wallet's directly held YT (two base units remain in index rounding). The frontend flow and final verdict payout remain pending until the live report matures; the deployed-state fork exercises those future phases.
- The curve is a bounded market price mechanism, not an oracle for future yield or the report verdict. Formal security review, adversarial testing, and real-yield liquidity design are still required before non-demo funds.

**The report matures 30 days after lock; real-time settlement cannot occur before then.**
