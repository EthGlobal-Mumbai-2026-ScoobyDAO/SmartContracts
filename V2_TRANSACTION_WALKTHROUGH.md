# V2 testnet transaction walkthrough

This is the live demo sequence. **Ethereum Sepolia** hosts the report, vault, Uniswap v4 YT market, and CCIP receiver. **Arbitrum Sepolia** hosts the CCIP sender. Amounts below use the six-decimal **DemoUSD (dUSD)** token, which is our test token, not Circle USDC. ETH paid gas and the CCIP fee; ETH was not the transferred asset. All links point to onchain transactions unless labeled as a test or CCIP message.

The signing wallet was `0xd561d3e95a02fDD8DbC94d15BFBF1b1634234b3a`. The complete contract address list is in [v2-testnets.json](deployments/v2-testnets.json).

1. **Prepare the CCIP token on both chains.** Deploy dUSD and Chainlink BurnMintTokenPool on [Ethereum Sepolia: dUSD](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xeda54cca80cd8adfb5fddd41e74d2c29b60876c9da18a72507e2e43719a86a1c), [Ethereum Sepolia: pool](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x5f65df42042d265c4de66a2e57c8232cad2b62d58496aabda7487b827b81625f), [Arbitrum Sepolia: dUSD](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0x15a67b0347304e07fbf7832f8b1706234eb12907fe48aed9c1926c975a6e6e46), and [Arbitrum Sepolia: pool](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0x49dff7a5232bec05a232aab99bec20de0ee8d55522d181a42c40ad810508dee7). Configure the remote pool on [Ethereum Sepolia](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x8681f2e037592f61067167b56f3b978a5785cdc7608769f67b7a2cf57e1cd62a) and [Arbitrum Sepolia](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0xa633a00c1b1a32791db72e47e1e0883ea1960d37f3f05a2283cf61cdc9c7b03a), then activate each pool in its token admin registry on [Ethereum Sepolia](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x5d8ed89470b15cb5856dd1c191d080d3589baca2aabdf81935587d45f8b5c3bb) and [Arbitrum Sepolia](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0xad005662e01ceec7853ec5e1bcc83150d8c537e94b1af5c8cec3803767d0b917). These shared token contracts are reused by V2.

2. **Deploy V2 voting on Ethereum Sepolia.** Deploy the [ReportRegistry](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x7a1df17d084990f3a437f712732e23cac66cbfa4f26cd1665923562cd9613db8), [RiskFiStakeVaultV2 and REP receipt](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xd6cea49b62d2300cb87abb91d6ec84339886d5561fa53739bf02cb5673829927), and [CCIPStakeReceiverV2](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xd5e4a61096543441b628abb701b185e1284e8b69bd2d62a0c79f8f032e584bba). REP represents collateral before it is staked.

3. **Deploy and connect the cross-chain sender.** Deploy [CCIPStakeSenderV2 on Arbitrum Sepolia](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0x45dc450447f96db6b4061aa304d79c6d473d8e372802e12cee1b99e266cfc7b0). The Ethereum Sepolia receiver [allowlists that sender](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xb3a502e143c8eecb2bf939f8ffda57e56cfab087da39901f95a4abbd0de56330).

4. **Publish the report and start its yield series.** A reviewer signature authorizes [report #1 publication](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x4ca1797532d3836d0942be6ac20d87052c6fe873d245009c7bb006671a3df1cd). The vault [initializes the series and creates its StakerYieldToken (YT)](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x83e066eb19dda2e30f54d3114a360ade498f53d87bd74e6ed38185c8c03f480f). The Arbitrum sender [sets the same report's cross-chain cutoff](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0x959948f9f24fd0945fb160c944c83f285e66a1e2cc06d411467fa19b1ea816ca). The cutoff is **November 8, 2026, 07:44 UTC**; the local stake lock is **November 9, 2026, 07:44 UTC**.

5. **Create the Uniswap v4 YT market.** Deploy the [V2 curve factory](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x8487e356278437e4c6d9ab4f8685fbe5db5e32dfc0b16cec18fd90c0b58a1802). It mines and [deploys the v4 hook, creates LP shares, and initializes the pool](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x61b15809af0f0af4af23e099afe327c76fc6c6c2afdf3b35c8c896798fa7808c). Deploy the [swap router](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xc85c657725fe7e0404854d08602331fc4adffae6bc7656af03c2ae4493089400) and [atomic stake-and-sell router](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xc36c5f6000d0fdecb32f492c9518a36db268de164840a9ec0217569d5eeabb65).

6. **Seed the Yes side and LP inventory.** The wallet [stakes 100 dUSD Yes](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x011dd447c03c1ea8ff8025e72e30a1bef77eeec7299bc493911c60364262e0d0). The vault records the verdict position and mints 100 YT. The wallet then [supplies 50 dUSD and 100 YT to the hook](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x076b98e0b8a1aeb43ded81737de69bc09a2ea11830e6ec4466bd3850b82e493f) and receives LP shares. The Yes position stays with the wallet.

7. **Trade YT in the live v4 pool.** The wallet [buys YT with 0.1 dUSD](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x5e1bf9e88d2cfe91752b076334058670ecdb874909ecd0663aa6678c9b737027), then [sells that YT back](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xdb2a2da2e3097db60a3a334622ccb72ab9839b52bb284acb0e171a1f8dbbab25). The hook's time-aware rate curve and custom-accounting swap run through the deployed v4 PoolManager.

8. **Stake and sell YT atomically.** One [Ethereum Sepolia transaction](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x8f5e454b971a1b3a19a684b1ca47bcd1916250b706148329456292e8e7a0a63a) stakes 10 dUSD No, mints 10 YT to the atomic router, sells that YT into the v4 pool, and returns the sale proceeds to the wallet. The wallet keeps its 10 dUSD No verdict position.

9. **Demonstrate yield before the cross-chain vote arrives.** The wallet [injects 10 dUSD of simulated yield](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xe837d7ff95efc98d4921a7c3bdd2ad50d12b29c57bd862e21c8f4cef2176c9ec). The LP [claims 9.999977 dUSD](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x7fc2d0effd7f918e72022f1f1f5bc485da7f5d7ca82802836751aead261e4568); the wallet's small directly held YT balance [claims 0.000021 dUSD](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x364ab559d495f4f72136c3e50932c58f3e11b9804cffd8dca8fd6462c4c1332c). This yield is simulated collateral income.

10. **Send the cross-chain funded No vote.** On Arbitrum Sepolia, the wallet [sends 5 dUSD plus V2 vote data through Chainlink CCIP](https://sepolia.arbiscan.io/tx/0x4f4e9a9f44ce28fb65ca80c5ba19a6ccda56223156e6e90d33884ffc18a99de2). The dUSD is burned on the source chain for minting on the destination; Arbitrum Sepolia ETH pays the native CCIP fee. Track message [`0xaed819…c4db1456d`](https://ccip.chain.link/#/side-drawer/msg/0xaed819aad1fde53644aa058481a0f606f0ae2e9f78a9bbf2a3264e8c4db1456d). The send alone does not show destination credit.

11. **Recover the destination execution.** Chainlink's [first Ethereum Sepolia attempt](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x9e16a3dccbb9215668b02b55ab7391b4d775bda8bf9ac582a0cf66d199401ea0) recorded `FAILURE` with an empty-data `ReceiverError`, consistent with insufficient receiver gas. A [manual retry with higher receiver and outer transaction gas](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x9402e5d2292c1e0f3862678fc8e86eec1d479013a5d0dd0c08a25ccc6f38b9b1) recorded **CCIP SUCCESS**. The receiver marked the message delivered, the wallet's No position rose from 10 to **15 dUSD**, and its YT balance rose by **5 YT** to **5.000232 YT**. Future sends default to a 1,500,000 receiver callback gas limit.

12. **Show yield on the CCIP-minted YT.** After delivery, another [10 dUSD simulated yield injection](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x373def2303b31624062f066bcc7d1f9c64a038aa9c4519c66e15e79d93b103e4) paid [9.565196 dUSD to the LP](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0xb89ea488c1067e47ac33f727b4b834124a33bebfce71852851813241b98b8374) and [0.434802 dUSD to the wallet holding the cross-chain YT](https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x2ec3844e8ddfe4627488fadad6a3ab57dd8543125b936168ee96ec550d64039d). The late-arriving YT did not collect yield from step 9.

13. **Test verdict and payout at future maturity.** The live report matures **December 9, 2026, 07:44 UTC**, so it has no live settlement transaction yet. [`V2DeployedFork.t.sol`](test/V2DeployedFork.t.sol) reads the deployed contracts and credited CCIP position, advances only a local Sepolia fork to maturity, then tests proposal, challenge, adjudication, principal claims, the protocol fee, and LP exit. This simulation sends **no** transaction to either testnet. The full `forge test --summary` suite passed **29 tests**.
